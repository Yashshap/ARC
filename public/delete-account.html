Google Play Data Safety & Deletion

VitalSync (ARC) — Account & Data Deletion

App Package: com.vitalsync.health • Developer: Yash

Method 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the VitalSync (ARC) app on your device.
  2. Tap your Profile avatar in the top header bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (gear icon) button in the top right corner.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete Everything.

What gets deleted immediately: Your Google Sign-In session token, user profile metrics, water hydration logs, meal & macro/micronutrient logs, custom meals, workout plans/sessions, and skincare/supplement logs stored in IndexedDB and local device storage.

Method 2: Revoke Google Account Access & Clear Device Data

  1. On your Android phone, open Settings → Apps → VitalSync → Storage & cache and tap Clear storage / Clear data (or uninstall the app).
  2. To revoke Google OAuth Sign-In permissions from the web at any time, visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, select VitalSync, and click Remove Access.
