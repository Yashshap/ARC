VitalSync (ARC) — Privacy Policy

Package ID: com.vitalsync.health • Effective Date: September 26, 2026

1. Medical, Nutrition & Fitness Disclaimer

VitalSync (ARC) is designed exclusively for general fitness, hydration logging, dietary macro/micronutrient tracking, workout timing, and personal wellness organization. VitalSync is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatment. Calculated BMI values, daily calorie/macronutrient targets, micronutrient Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) percentages, and pill/supplement reminders are informational estimates only. Always consult a licensed physician, pharmacist, or registered dietitian before beginning any nutrition plan, supplement regimen, or workout program.

2. On-Device Data Storage & Android Auto-Backup

VitalSync is built on a strict local-first architecture:

  • All health metrics, water logs, logged meals, custom meal recipes, workout routines, and skincare/supplement schedules are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB and local application storage.
  • We do not operate external cloud databases that collect, store, or profile your personal health or dietary history.
  • On Android devices, standard Android OS Auto-Backup (android:allowBackup="true") may back up your local application snapshot to your personal, encrypted Google Drive device backup if device backup is enabled in your Android system settings.

3. Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) Data Handling

You may use VitalSync in Guest Mode without creating an account or signing in. If you optionally sign in using Google Sign-In:

  • We request read-only access to your basic Google account profile: Display Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture URL.
  • This information is stored locally on your device solely to personalize your in-app profile header and isolate your local device database (ArcHealth_<userId>).
  • We never transmit your Google identity or health logs to third-party servers, and we never request access to your Google Contacts, Gmail, Calendar, or Drive files.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising or Tracking

VitalSync contains zero third-party advertising networks, behavioral analytics SDKs, data brokers, or cross-app tracking identifiers. We never sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

5. Data Portability, Retention & Account Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you have complete control at all times:

  • Export Data: You can export a complete JSON backup of your records from your Profile/Settings screen at any time.
  • In-App Account & Data Deletion: Open the app and navigate to Profile → Settings → Delete Account to permanently and immediately wipe your local IndexedDB database, snapshot files, and active Google session.
  • Web Deletion Instructions: Detailed step-by-step instructions for deleting your account and data are available at https://vitalsync.health/delete-account.html.

6. GDPR & DPDP Privacy Rights

In compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), and Google Play User Data policies, we practice strict data minimization. Users may exercise their rights to access, rectify, export, or erase their data directly within the app at any time with zero waiting period.
